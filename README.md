Started this project on Sept 24 Idea is coming from it was a day our great class teacher vishal sir was assigned us ppt presentation that time they told us to you have to give ppt presentation for building your presentation skill and confidence among crowd and have to build strong communication at that time i was saw one problem everyone was doing ppt or pdf slide changes by up and down button of laptop then thats breaking the flow of presentation stuff so i got one idea why should we not do one thing let's do it on gestures and make it touchless and also with AI and modern design. so it will not break the flow of presentation and started building this project.

A modern, touchless presentation controller powered by **React 18**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **MediaPipe Hands** for real-time AI gesture recognition.

---

## ✨ Features

- **✋ AI Gesture Control**:
  - `✋ Open Palm`: Advance to next slide
  - `✊ Fist`: Go back to previous slide
  - `☝️ Index Up`: Jump directly to the first slide
  - `🤙 Call-Me / Shaka`: Jump directly to the last slide
- **📄 High-DPI PDF Rendering**: Powered by PDF.js with responsive aspect ratio scaling and slide transition animations.
- **🎨 Glassmorphic Tailwind UI**: Dark-mode aesthetic with ambient gradient lighting, auto-hiding navigation bars, and slide thumbnail previews.
- **📷 Collapsible Camera HUD**: Live webcam skeleton overlay showing detected hand joints and connection lines in real-time.
- **⚡ Keyboard Navigation**: Supports Arrow keys, Space, Home, End, Esc, and F (fullscreen toggle).
